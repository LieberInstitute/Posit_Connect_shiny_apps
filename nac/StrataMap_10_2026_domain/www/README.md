A Shiny app for nucleus accumbens (NAc) cells from donors Br6522, Br3943,
and Br3899. Under **spot-level data**, use **Interactive** to zoom into
registered histology and inspect cell outlines, or **Static grid** to compare
donors. **Layer-level data** uses pseudobulk profiles grouped by donor and
BANKSY spatial domain (`domains_1_05`).
